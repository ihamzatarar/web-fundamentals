What you should have seen: a blank page for 5 seconds, then this heading in blue. You never saw red, even though index.css (red) arrived first.

Why: the browser won't paint until every stylesheet in the head has arrived, because a later file can override an earlier one (the cascade). That is render-blocking.

Check in Network: index.css finishes at once, index2.css takes ~5 s, and nothing was painted until it finished.
